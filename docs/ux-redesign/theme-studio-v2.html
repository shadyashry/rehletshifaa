Theme Studio v2: five visual worlds for an international health-travel platform

The first Theme Studio (theme-studio.html) is re-read here with the frontend-design plugin, Impeccable's new-work method and the ui-ux-pro-max checklist, then regenerated: the four worlds are rendered again with the generated-design tells removed, a fifth world is added and built out as the full comp (home hero, My Care current step, a coordinator case page header, logo lockups, English and Arabic, phone and desktop), and a token proposal closes the page. Nothing here invents claims; the case number and the patient are marked as examples.

Every text pair on this page was measured against WCAG AA (4.5:1 for text, 3:1 for component edges and marks); the numbers are printed beside the colours and collected in the appendix.

What the plugins changed in the reading

The frontend-design plugin keeps a list of the traits generated pages cluster around. Read against it, the first studio has four of them in the comp every world shares, and the incumbent world carries a fifth:

  • Eyebrow above the heading, opened by a coral rule. The "signature" of Petrol & Paper is the most common template chrome there is; Impeccable's craft floor lists the kicker as a ban, not a default. v2 removes it from every world; the letterhead in world E carries the context instead.
  • Middle-dot meta strings ("Credentials reviewed · current") and an arrow appended to the button ("Send my case →"). Both removed.
  • Tracked uppercase labels on the page keys ("PRELIMINARY ESTIMATE") and the stamp. Removed; labels are sentence case in both scripts, which is also the only setting Arabic can share.
  • Warm cream ground with a terracotta accent is trait number one on the list: Petrol & Paper's paper (#f7f4ee) with logo coral (#e07a5f) sits inside it, and the coral itself fails 3:1 (2.69) so it cannot even draw a focus ring or a journey marker ring under WCAG 1.4.11. World D's mint has the same defect (2.53). A mark colour that fails non-text contrast is decoration only; it cannot be the ring of a reached step.
  • Near-black with one bright accent is trait two: B (petrol night with aqua) and D (ink buttons with mint) are its two renditions.
  • A real defect: the first studio ships without <!doctype>, <meta charset> or a viewport meta. Served over HTTP, or opened as a file in a browser that defaults to Windows-1252, every Arabic block renders as mojibake. v2 declares all three.
  • Arabic as the second comp. All four worlds set the Latin hero first and mirror it; Arabic is a smaller step of the Latin scale (Cairo set smaller than Jakarta). Owner rule 3 asks for the opposite, and world E is built from the Arabic page outward.

ui-ux-pro-max was consulted as the checklist owner, never as the look. Its design-system match for "medical travel coordination" returned Neumorphism, a cyan palette and a testimonials carousel: the category default, and it conflicts with PRODUCT.md (no testimonials, no soft-shadow panels). Its pre-delivery checklist (44px targets, visible focus, reduced motion, 4.5:1, no emoji as icons, 375/768/1024/1440) is applied to the comp below. Its Google Fonts index confirmed the Arabic faces considered (Noto Naskh Arabic, Readex Pro, Almarai, Markazi Text).

E, new in v2, built out as the comp: the bilingual clinic letterhead

Letterhead & Seal

Every family in Benghazi, Khartoum, Baghdad or Sanaa who has sought care in Egypt already holds the artefact this world is built from: an Egyptian doctor's letterhead, Arabic on the right, English on the left, the practice mark in the centre, a double rule, the report beneath, and a violet seal where something was done. Bond white instead of cream, one blue-black ink for text and actions, one seal colour reserved for the stamp and the focus ring, a Naskh display face whose Latin was drawn to match the Arabic, and Readex Pro for everything you read on a phone. The product is a case file; the letterhead is how a case file says who stands behind it.

How this world was chosen

Impeccable new-work, step 1 (what is already true)
An established world with a DESIGN.md, reopened by the owner. Pinned: the dossier and the stamp, no fabrication, AA, Arabic first, no gradients, no gold, accents only as marks. The mark is open.
Step 2 (what would change the work)
The owner was not at the keyboard; the brief answers the mode questions. Persuade on the home: a family member on a phone must believe that one accountable person holds the file and a Consultant reviews it before any money moves; the proof is the file itself. Operate on My Care and the staff page: the current step and the next action, nothing else.
Step 3 (seven grounded candidates from the audience's world, by resonance)
1 the stamped treatment-abroad referral file; 2 the ruled Arabic manuscript page with its marginal gloss (recommendation in the block, coordinator's note in the margin); 3 the bilingual clinic letterhead; 4 the lab report with reference ranges; 5 Cairo airport wayfinding and the boarding stub; 6 mashrabiya light on limestone; 7 the WhatsApp thread (a tool the audience operates, spent as the one allowed). The category's own page (teal telehealth with a doctor photo) and its opposite (dark concierge) were kept out of the list as the rut.
The roll
impeccable concept-seed --scope direction --mode persuade, seed 7766245a, assigned candidate 3. Challengers dealt: city-pop airbrush sleeve (declined: gradients and chrome are banned by the owner), linocut broadside (declined; it donates one ink, total commitment: text, rules and buttons share a single ink), algorave code floor and phosphor terminal (declined; the terminal donates states print themselves as words, never as chrome: the staff header types "Consultant review in progress" instead of a coloured pill), brick build instructions (competitive on product clarity; it donates only the next piece is bright, the finished ones are ghosted, applied to the journey steps), Miura-fold sheet (declined; its one-module grid is already the letterhead's rule). Impeccable's own pick would have been candidate 1, the stamped referral file, which world C already renders; its honest risk is familiarity. The decision board the method serves was replaced by this page because the session ran unattended; the owner's gate is below.

What it says

"The doctor's own letterhead, with your name on it." Official without being a ministry; Egyptian without a symbol; bilingual by construction, because the letterhead always was.

Where it wins

  • Arabic is structurally first: the Naskh face leads, the Latin was drawn to follow it, and the letterhead keeps Arabic on the right in every locale.
  • The bilingual header doubles as the language switch, so the one chrome element every page needs becomes the signature.
  • Two colours and white; every pair measured; the seal passes 3:1 as a mark and 4.5:1 as text, so rings, focus and stamp all meet AA.
  • White bond, blue-black ink and a violet seal sit outside every trait on the plugin's list, and outside the category's teal.

Honest risks

  • A serif-flavoured Naskh display on the web is a stronger voice than a geometric sans; in body sizes it must never be used (Readex Pro does that work).
  • Violet reads "stamp" to this audience; to a European eye it may read "finance". It is never a surface, so the risk stays small.
  • Rename the ramp: "petrol" tokens would carry ink values. The proposal below keeps the names where code depends on them and adds the new ones.

Cost to adopt

Medium-high, in three sessions: tokens, fonts and type scale with DESIGN.md; mark, lockups, favicon, Keycloak and WhatsApp; screenshot baselines, a11y, Playwright, Control Center convergence. Pre-production with no data to keep (owner, 2026-09-26) is the cheapest moment there will ever be.

Recommendation

Adopt E, Letterhead & Seal, with the new mark. It is the only world of the five that answers all three things the owner reopened at once: it is distinctive for the right reason (the audience's own artefact, not a palette trick), it designs Arabic as the first voice in the type itself, and it gives the mark a meaning the product can explain in one sentence. It also clears the plugin's list without exception, which neither A nor B can do while they keep cream, coral and the eyebrow.

For this audience: a family deciding on treatment abroad trusts paper with a letterhead and a seal more than it trusts an app, and it reads Naskh faster than Kufi or a geometric sans. For this business: the product's claim is "a Consultant reviews your file before you commit"; a letterhead is what makes a review a document, and the seal is what makes a step real. For the staff: one ink, typed states and a ruled header make a denser working screen than any coloured-pill system, and it is the same world as the patient's page.
