# How leading hospitals' international patient offices handle first contact and coordinator assignment

Research date: 2026-10-10. Most facts come from the hospitals' own pages or search snippets of them, fetched or indexed in 2025–2026, unless flagged. Several official pages (Memorial, Johns Hopkins appointments page, Apollo IPS, Fortis international landing page) returned HTTP 403 to the fetcher. For those, the facts come from search-result snippets of the official URLs and are marked that way.

## Q1. Which first-contact channels does each hospital expose, and is WhatsApp one official number, per-language numbers, or per-coordinator numbers?

### Takeaway
None of the hospitals checked publishes per-coordinator WhatsApp numbers on its site. The common pattern is one official WhatsApp number per hospital, per brand site, or per city/facility, alongside a phone line, a web inquiry or call-back form and email. The most language-specific setups are phone lines (Anadolu has 8 per-language lines; Vejthani has an Arabic WhatsApp/hotline). Bumrungrad and Fortis also run regional or overseas referral and information offices with local mobile/WhatsApp numbers. The US and German academic centers (Mayo, Johns Hopkins, Cleveland Clinic US, Charité) publish no WhatsApp channel; they use web forms, email and phone.

No hospital publishes individual coordinators' WhatsApp numbers. Any per-coordinator numbers appear only after the case starts (see Q2/Q4).
- *Inference:* in India and Egypt, many WhatsApp numbers found online belong to **facilitators/aggregators** (My 1Health, Vaidam, Lyfboat, Karetrip), not the hospital. A platform like RehletShifaa competes in exactly this layer.

## Q2. Do they promise a dedicated coordinator / case manager, and at what step (and by language, country or specialty) is the coordinator assigned?

### Takeaway
Most programs promise a single point of contact. The explicit "dedicated coordinator, single point of contact throughout" wording appears at Cleveland Clinic Abu Dhabi and Cleveland Clinic London, and Johns Hopkins promises a "care coordinator" as the first point of contact. Where the timing is stated, assignment happens **after the formal request or intake form**, not at the first chat or call. Hopkins sends a coordinator email after the form; Mayo staff contact you within 2–3 business days. Language and region are the organizing axes (Bumrungrad's Arabic vs International teams; Bangkok Hospital AMS; Anadolu's language lines). Specialty matters for clinical review, not coordinator assignment.

### Cited Findings
- Cleveland Clinic Abu Dhabi: "You will be introduced to your dedicated global patient services coordinator, who will remain the single point of contact throughout your journey." The page does not say when this happens. It also mentions bilingual coordinators and "escort ambassadors". — [CCAD International Patient Services](https://clevelandclinicabudhabi.ae/international-patients/international-patient-services)
- Cleveland Clinic London: "The GPS team will be your single point of contact throughout your time with us". Note this is a team, not one named person. — [Cleveland Clinic London GPS](https://clevelandcliniclondon.uk/patients/global-patient-services)
- Cleveland Clinic Florida: prospective patients can "speak with a Global Patient Services Coordinator" by phone before registering. — [Cleveland Clinic Florida international FAQ](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/florida/help/international-patients-faq)
- Johns Hopkins International: after the International Patient Interest Form / appointment request is submitted, "a care coordinator emails you" about your condition and goals. The coordinator is the "first point of contact for scheduling, billing, interpretation, and medical records". Source is the search snippet of the official page. — [JHI – What to expect next](https://www.hopkinsmedicine.org/international/patients/appointments)
- Johns Hopkins also employs nurse-level "Case Manager – International" (International Nurse Liaison) staff, which separates clinical case management from logistics coordination. — [JH jobs – Case Manager International](https://jobs.hopkinsmedicine.org/job/95111/case-manager-international-nursing-us-md-baltimore-johns-hopkins-health-system)
- Mayo Clinic: there is no named coordinator promise. "A member of the team will usually contact you within two business days". Staff may request records "to better understand your condition and tailor your visit". Scheduling takes 1–2 days, followed by a confirmation letter, and Financial Services contacts the patient before the visit. International Services staff "arrange appointments, find interpreters, guide you in making travel and lodging arrangements, and answer questions about billing and insurance." — [Mayo – appointment process](https://www.mayoclinic.org/international/appointment-process); [Mayo – International patients billing](https://mayoclinic.org/billing-insurance/before-arrival/international-patients)
- Bumrungrad Medical Coordination Office (free service): an **Arabic team of 8 coordination physicians + 3 nurse coordinators** and an **International team of 9 coordination physicians + 1 nurse + 1 officer** covering 30+ countries and 10+ languages. Named units also exist for Myanmar, Cambodia, China and SAARC. Before arrival, coordinators review records for an initial specialist recommendation, give cost estimates and schedule appointments and telemedicine. During the stay they round on inpatients and join family meetings. After discharge they prepare reports, arrange repatriation and fit-to-fly, and make follow-up calls. The page does **not** state per-patient assignment or its timing. — [Bumrungrad MCO](https://bumrungrad.com/en/patient-services/medical-coordination-office)
- Acibadem: promises "dedicated support from first consultation through recovery" and refers to a "patient coordinator" replying on WhatsApp. It does not state when a named coordinator is assigned. Initial case review is free. — [Acibadem International](https://acibademinternational.com/?p=208)
- Fortis (Mumbai): "an International Patient Coordinator will liaise between you and the hospital". Official steps are general enquiry, then "medical opinion by experts" with an indicative cost from shared reports, then a final estimate after physical evaluation (from search snippets of official pages). — [Fortis Mumbai international](https://www.fortishealthcare.com/lp/fortis-hospital-mumbai-brand-international); [Fortis international patients](https://fortishealthcare.com/ru/international-patients)
- Medanta: "Dedicated assistant for patients support before, during and post-treatment". Interpreters cover Russian, Arabic, Burmese, Uzbek, Dari/Pashto and French. Timing is not stated. — [Medanta International Patient Help Desk](https://www.medanta.org/international-patient-help-desk)
- Mount Elizabeth: the Patient Assistance Centre provides the service at no additional cost. No per-patient coordinator promise was found in the snippets. — [Mount Elizabeth International Patients Guide](https://www.mountelizabeth.com.sg/international-patients-guide)
- Saudi German (international page, Dubai-branded): "each international patient is supported by a dedicated care coordinator". — [Saudi German – international patients](https://www.saudigerman.com/ar/international-patients/)

### Inferences
- *Inference:* the industry pattern is a two-step model. (1) A pooled intake/contact team, often segmented by language or region, handles first contact. (2) A named coordinator owns the case once a formal request or intake form exists, usually around medical records review and quote. Clinical review by physicians (Bumrungrad's "coordination physicians", Fortis's "medical opinion by experts", Hopkins's nurse case managers) is a separate role from the logistics coordinator.
- *Inference:* "dedicated coordinator" in marketing sometimes means a team (Cleveland Clinic London) rather than one person. A product that guarantees one named person is differentiating, but must define cover for absences.

### Gaps
- No hospital publicly states whether the coordinator is assigned by country, by language, or round-robin within a language team. Bumrungrad's team structure implies language/region.
- Timing of CCAD's coordinator introduction, and whether it happens before or after records review, was not stated.

## Q3. How do they handle volume: contact centers, response SLAs, chatbots, CRMs?

### Takeaway
Published SLAs cluster around **"within 24 h" (Turkish/Indian/facilitator sites) and "2–3 business days" (US academic centers)**. The best-documented technical setup is Bumrungrad's. Per vendor Bird, all LINE/WhatsApp/Messenger chats land in one omnichannel inbox, with AI language detection routing them to Thai, English, Japanese and Arabic teams, and each chat becomes a ticket synced to a **Salesforce case** as the "single source of truth". Hospitals use chatbots mostly for FAQ, appointment and triage front-ends with human handover. No public source showed a chatbot replacing coordinators for international patients.

### Cited Findings
- **Bumrungrad + Bird (vendor case study, undated):** a welcome message with options, then routing to a representative. AI language detection routes to dedicated **Thai, English, Japanese and Arabic** teams, with Chinese planned. Each chat becomes a ticket in Bird Inbox synced to a matching **Salesforce case**, and closing a ticket auto-updates case status. Claimed outcome: "Bumrungrad no longer misses a single patient interaction". No volumes or SLA figures are given. Planned additions are sentiment-analysis bots, multilingual FAQ bots and AI translation for agents. Context: 1.1M patients/yr from 190+ countries. — [Bird – Bumrungrad](https://bird.com/zh-sg/kehu/bumrungrad-international-hospital/)
- **Mayo Clinic SLA:** "usually contact you within two business days" on one page. Another Mayo page says an international appointment office "will respond to your request within three business days". These two pages are internally inconsistent. — [Mayo – appointment process](https://www.mayoclinic.org/international/appointment-process); [Mayo – appointments](https://mayoclinic.org/departments-centers/international/appointments)
- **Acibadem SLA claims, internally inconsistent:** WhatsApp is "typically within minutes", but a stat on the same page says a typical first reply takes about 2 h. Messages take "a few hours" and appointment requests are confirmed within 24 h. Phone and WhatsApp are listed as 24/7. An "Ask Aida" chat widget captures leads for advisor follow-up. — [Acibadem International](https://acibademinternational.com/?p=208)
- **Mount Elizabeth:** the WhatsApp line is staffed only Mon–Fri 9–5, so after-hours volume goes to the 24-hour phone hotline. — [Mount Elizabeth FAQ](https://www.mountelizabeth.com.sg/frequently-asked-questions)
- **CCAD:** the international team works Mon–Fri 8–5 GST, with a separate 24/7 mobile line. — [CCAD International Patient Services](https://clevelandclinicabudhabi.ae/international-patients/international-patient-services)
- **Cleopatra (CHG):** app bookings are confirmed by a central call center "within minutes". — [CHG app](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1441433048)
- **Hospital Alemão Oswaldo Cruz (Brazil), Infobip:** WhatsApp is used for scheduling consultations, exams and check-ups. Use cases are automated with **automatic transfer to human agents** for complex cases. An Infobip–**HubSpot** integration lets staff send WhatsApp/SMS from the CRM. — [Infobip – Hospital Alemão Oswaldo Cruz](https://www.infobip.com/customer/hospital-alemao-oswaldo-cruz)
- **Mediclinic (SA/CH/UAE), Infobip + Microsoft Azure Bot Service (2021, COVID-era):** a WhatsApp screening bot, working model in 2 days and launched a week later. This is not international-patient intake. — [Microsoft CEE news (2021)](https://news.microsoft.com/en-cee/2021/11/02/infobip-makes-hospital-check-ins-faster-and-safer-with-azure-bot-service/)
- **Johns Hopkins Aramco Healthcare:** a WhatsApp chatbot for MyChart appointment changes and medical-liaison requests, with escalation to a human representative. — [JHAH WhatsApp Service](https://www.jhah.com/en/contact-us/whatsapp-service/)
- **Medical-tourism facilitators:** Praga Medica (Prague) moved international-patient inquiries to the WhatsApp Business API after finding email too slow and webchat spam-heavy (vendor respond.io). MDabroad chose WhatsApp with LivePerson to reach overseas patients regardless of country or carrier. — [respond.io – WhatsApp for healthcare](https://respond.io/blog/whatsapp-for-healthcare); [LivePerson – MDabroad](https://liveperson.com/resources/success-stories/mdabroad)
- A Route Mobile case study describes a Gulf/UAE hospital that integrated a WhatsApp chatbot for real-time notifications and alerts (hospital unnamed). — [Route Mobile – hospital case study](https://routemobile.com/case-studies/hospital-healthcare-sector/)
- Apollo 24|7's WhatsApp symptom checker reportedly runs on Infobip, but only a third-party blog says so (**unverified**). Gupshup has no public hospital case study. Its healthcare page describes bots that "transfer them to the appropriate department". — search summary of [Gupshup resources](https://www.gupshup.ai/resources/?p=16178)

### Inferences
- *Inference:* the reference architecture for volume is one official WhatsApp Business number (or several per facility) feeding a shared inbox (Bird, respond.io, Infobip Conversations and similar). A bot handles greeting, language and intent capture, then routing to a language team. Every conversation is mirrored into a CRM case (Salesforce at Bumrungrad, HubSpot at Oswaldo Cruz), and the case, not the phone, is the unit of continuity.
- *Inference:* SLAs published by Turkish and facilitator sites (minutes to 24 h) are much tighter than US academic centers' (2–3 business days). Arab-market patients likely compare against the former.

### Gaps
- No primary source on CRM choice for Acibadem, Memorial, Apollo, Medanta, Fortis, CCAD or the Egyptian groups. No Salesforce Health Cloud or Microsoft Dynamics case study naming one of these hospitals' international offices was found in this pass.
- No measured response-time or volume data (inquiries/day, agents per language) was found for any listed hospital.

## Q4. Public evidence (vendor case studies, job postings, interviews, reviews) about routing and keeping one coordinator per patient

### Takeaway
Job postings, especially Turkish and Dubai medical-tourism and clinic roles, show coordinators running prospective patients over **WhatsApp, Instagram/Meta, phone and email**, logging leads in a **CRM**, and carrying **sales/conversion targets, sometimes with commission**. They own the patient "from first inquiry through post-op follow-up". This is how one-coordinator-per-patient works in practice. The only routing evidence for a major hospital is the Bumrungrad/Bird case study (language-based team routing plus Salesforce case per chat). It routes to a team, not explicitly to a sticky individual.

### Cited Findings
- Istanbul aesthetic-surgery coordinator posting (since removed): it says to "communicate directly with international patients through WhatsApp, Instagram, email, and phone". It covers the full journey (consultation, scheduling, hospital coordination, treatment, post-op follow-up) and supports sales conversion. — [kariyer.net posting](https://docs.kariyer.net/is-ilani/mehmet-bekir-sen-klinigi-experienced-health-tourism-patient-coordinator-3949347)
- Turkish hospital medical-tourism coordinator: uses an "online CRM system", guides patients hotel to hospital, arranges consultations and transfers, and reports weekly/monthly to management. Another Istanbul posting ties the role to monthly sales targets. — [kariyer.net – Medkor patient coordinator](https://www.kariyer.net/is-ilani/medkor-saglik-ve-bilisim-hizmetleri-ltd-sti-patient-coordinator-3186695); [yenibiris – Italian-speaking patient coordinator](https://www.yenibiris.com/is-ilani/italian-patient-coordinator-italyanca-bilen-saglik-turizmi-uzmani/980890)
- Language-specific coordinator hiring, e.g. an "Italian Patient Coordinator", shows Turkish providers staff coordinators per language market. — [yenibiris posting](https://www.yenibiris.com/is-ilani/italian-patient-coordinator-italyanca-bilen-saglik-turizmi-uzmani/980890)
- Dubai clinic patient-coordinator postings: outreach "through phone, WhatsApp, Meta channels, and email", leads "nurtured through CRM systems", and monthly and quarterly enrollment and revenue targets. Retention re-engagement is logged in the CRM. — [GulfTalent posting 605620](https://www.gulftalent.com/uae/jobs/patient-coordinator-605620); [GulfTalent posting 624024](https://www.gulftalent.com/uae/jobs/patient-coordinator-624024)
- An international patient coordinator posting (GRS Recruitment) exists in the same results; its details were not fetched. — [GRS Recruitment](https://jobs.grsrecruitment.com/job/international-patient-coordinator-11466.aspx)
- Johns Hopkins "Case Manager – International" (nursing) postings show that US academic centers pair a coordinator with a nurse case manager. — [JH jobs](https://jobs.hopkinsmedicine.org/job/96104/case-manager-international-nursing-us-md-baltimore-johns-hopkins-hospital)
- Bumrungrad/Bird: conversations "are categorized so they reach the most suitable representative". This is team/skill routing, and the case study does not say whether the same agent stays sticky across conversations. — [Bird – Bumrungrad](https://bird.com/zh-sg/kehu/bumrungrad-international-hospital/)
- Cleveland Clinic GPS (dated, 2018): served ~8,000 patients from 90+ countries/yr, with in-country representatives. — [Cayman article 2018](https://tower.com.ky/2018/05/second-opinion-can-make-difference); [Cleveland Clinic GPS locations](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/patients/international/locations)

### Inferences
- *Inference:* in Turkish and Gulf private hospitals and clinics, the "one coordinator per patient" model often depends on the coordinator's own work WhatsApp handset or account plus a CRM log, and the role is partly sales-driven. This creates continuity but also risks: data leaving the official system, and patients lost when staff leave. No posting explicitly says "personal number", so this remains an inference from postings that list WhatsApp as the coordinator's personal working channel.
- *Inference:* large hospitals with omnichannel platforms (Bumrungrad) instead keep continuity at the CRM case level, routing by language/skill to a team. Whether a sticky owner is assigned is not public.

## Q5. What do hospitals' privacy notices say about using WhatsApp for medical information?

### Takeaway
None of the listed hospitals' privacy notices reviewed in this pass specifically addresses WhatsApp for medical information. Acibadem's forms take consent to process personal and health data and promise confidentiality, but say nothing about WhatsApp storage or encryption. Public-sector guidance (NHS trusts, Irish HSE, HIPAA commentary) is cautious to restrictive about patient data on consumer messaging, which gives a benchmark.

### Cited Findings
- Acibadem: forms include consent to process personal and health data under its privacy notice. The page says data "stays strictly confidential and is used only to help you", and the Aida panel says data is "Never shared with third parties". Nothing covers WhatsApp's encryption or chat retention. The site is for non-urgent inquiries only. — [Acibadem International](https://acibademinternational.com/?p=208)
- University Hospitals Bristol NHS Trust has an instant messaging policy covering staff use of WhatsApp-type apps. It treats medical and demographic details as confidential and directs staff to the Trust's secure alternative (Careflow Connect). — [UH Bristol IM policy](https://www.uhbristol.nhs.uk/media/3784093/20-137_-_instantmessagingpolicy-2_0.pdf)
- Irish HSE security policy reportedly recommends "no transmission of patient data through such software". — [Medical Independent](https://medicalindependent.ie/in-the-news/latest-news/hse-seeks-international-advice-on-whatsapp-type-software)
- BMJ Innovations survey (reported 2018, dated): 97% of surveyed doctors sent patient information by instant messenger without consent, while 68% were concerned about doing so. — [Digital Health (2018)](https://www.digitalhealth.net/2018/02/whatsapp-doc-legal-and-practical-perspectives-of-using-mobile-messaging/)
- A South African study concluded confidential information should only be shared on WhatsApp with patient consent; the facility studied had no WhatsApp policy. — [UFS scholar repository](https://scholar.ufs.ac.za/handle/11660/12272)

### Inferences
- *Inference:* hospitals advertising WhatsApp as their primary international channel (Acibadem, CCAD, Mount Elizabeth) appear to rely on general consent-to-process language in forms and privacy notices, not WhatsApp-specific disclosures. A platform that explicitly says what may be sent over WhatsApp and redirects records uploads to a secure portal would be above the observed norm.

### Gaps
- Full privacy notices of Acibadem, Memorial, Bumrungrad, CCAD, Apollo and the Egyptian groups were not read in this pass. A WhatsApp clause may exist in documents not reviewed.
- No Egyptian (Law 151/2020) or UAE health-data guidance specific to WhatsApp was gathered here.
