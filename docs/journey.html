The end-to-end care journey

How a single patient case travels the platform — from an anonymous request, through identity, clinical review, a four-party proposal, and treatment, to closed follow-up. One shared map for coordinators, clinicians, operations, finance, credentialing, and engineering.

Coordination, not care. RehletShifaa is an intake-and-coordination service. It does not diagnose, provide emergency care, or replace professional medical advice. Proposal acceptance is never procedure-specific medical consent.

The shape of it

Five phases, one accountable case

Every case is created once and carries a single accountable coordinator from intake to close. Work moves through five phases; the case status advances only along explicit, audited transitions.

Phase 1

Intake

An anonymous request is captured with consent, documents are uploaded privately and scanned.

patient (anonymous)
Phase 2

Verified access & ownership

The patient checks status through a purpose-scoped link and on-demand code; a coordinator takes ownership and triages.

patient · coordinator
Phase 3

Clinical review

A verified consultant is assigned, reviews the case, and approves a versioned recommendation.

coordinator · doctor
Phase 4

Proposal & decision

Clinical → operations → finance → release, then the patient accepts, declines, or asks to revise.

coordinator · ops · finance · patient
Phase 5

Care delivery

Travel is coordinated, arrival confirmed, treatment delivered and discharged, follow-up closed.

operations · doctor · coordinator

Step by step

The seventeen moves that make the journey

Each step names who acts, what they do, the API that carries it, the state it lands the case in, and the control that guards it.

1
Patient · anonymous

Create & submit the request

An unauthenticated patient fills the intake form with explicit consent. A draft case and a provisional patient profile are created, then submitted.

POST /api/v1/casesPOST …/submitDRAFT → RECEIVEDconsent + Turnstile + rate limit
2
Patient · anonymous

Upload & scan documents

Files are uploaded straight to private storage via a short-lived presigned URL, then confirmed: size, declared type, and true file signature are checked, the object is quarantined and virus-scanned, and only then marked clean.

…/documents/presign…/documents/confirmMIME + signature + ClamAV + quotas
3
Patient · anonymous

Check status through verified access

Submission creates a random, pepper-hashed status link. An OTP is minted only when the patient requests access, then exchanged for a short-lived hashed grant. The link and grant are case-scoped, expiring, revocable, attempt-limited, rate-limited, and replay-protected. Verification does not change case status; coordinator ownership drives intake.

POST /api/v1/public/cases/{token}/request-access · /verify · /viewstatus unchangedlink + on-demand OTP
4
Coordinator

Take ownership

A coordinator claims the case from the shared intake queue, becoming its one accountable owner. A row-lock guarantees a single primary coordinator.

POST /api/v1/coordinator/cases/{id}/claimone active PRIMARY owner
5
Coordinator

Triage completeness

The coordinator verifies the case is complete, exchanging secure messages and raising blocking tasks. Incomplete cases loop to INFORMATION_REQUIRED and back until ready.

POST …/transition→ READY_FOR_CONSULTANT
6
Coordinator

Assign a verified consultant

The coordinator selects an available consultant from the managed directory. The profile must be VERIFIED and have a current verified credential. Operations and finance are assigned later, only when their proposal or travel stage needs access.

POST …/assignments→ CONSULTANT_ASSIGNMENT_PENDINGcurrent credential + availability
7
Doctor

Accept the assignment

The consultant accepts (or declines) the case. Declines end the assignment; acceptance opens the clinical workspace.

POST …/assignments/{id}?accept=→ CONSULTANT_REVIEW
8
Doctor

Write & approve the clinical review

The consultant records a versioned clinical review (suitability, treatment, risks, follow-up). Final approval requires fresh re-authentication and locks the version as the mandatory basis for any proposal.

POST …/reviews…/reviews/{id}/approve→ CLINICAL_RECOMMENDATION_READYstep-up re-auth (≤10 min)
9
Coordinator

Draft the proposal

Built strictly from an approved clinical review: included/excluded services, itemized pricing, terms, validity, and disclaimers. A new immutable version is created.

POST …/proposals→ PROPOSAL_PREPARATIONrequires APPROVED review
10
Operations

Confirm the operational plan

Operations confirms delivery and travel feasibility on the proposal version.

POST …/proposals/{v}/completeversion: OPERATIONS_COMPLETED
11
Finance

Approve commercial terms

Finance re-authenticates and approves the pricing and terms.

POST …/proposals/{v}/approve→ PROPOSAL_INTERNAL_APPROVALstep-up re-auth (≤10 min)
12
Coordinator

Release to the patient

The coordinator releases the finance-approved version, freezing an immutable HTML snapshot. Only released versions are ever visible to the patient.

POST …/proposals/{v}/release→ PATIENT_DECISIONrequires FINANCE_APPROVED
13
Patient

Verify & decide via secure link

Release mints a random, expiring, case-scoped secure link. Before any clinical detail or pricing is shown, the patient requests a one-time code (delivered to the already-verified contact) and exchanges it for a short-lived view grant — possession of the URL plus a typed name is never enough. The patient then accepts, declines, or requests a revision on the exact released version. Expired/superseded/already-decided versions are refused. An authenticated returning patient can instead decide in-portal with step-up re-auth.

POST …/public/proposals/{token}/request-access · /verify · /view · /decision→ ACCEPTED / DECLINED / REVISION_REQUESTEDlink + OTP · not medical consent
14
Patient

Activate the account (after acceptance)

On acceptance, a one-time activation invitation is sent to the verified contact. Activating links the existing provisional profile — and every related case — to the newly authenticated account automatically; no case UUID or code is re-entered. Activation failure never undoes the recorded acceptance.

POST /api/v1/patient/account/activateone-time token · replay-protected
15
Operations

Coordinate travel & confirm arrival

Visa, flights, reception, accommodation, and transport are planned; confirmed arrival advances the case.

PUT …/travelACCEPTED → TRAVEL_COORDINATION → ARRIVAL_CONFIRMED
16
Doctor

Deliver treatment & discharge

Treatment episodes record procedures, milestones, and complications. A discharge document (clean, from this case) plus explicit discharge readiness is required to close the episode.

POST …/treatment-episodes→ TREATMENT_IN_PROGRESS → DISCHARGED
17
DoctorCoordinator

Follow up & close

The doctor schedules a follow-up plan (mode, tests, instructions); the coordinator closes the journey after follow-up work is complete.

POST …/follow-ups→ FOLLOW_UP → CLOSED

A parallel track

Practitioner credentialing — the gate before step 6

A doctor cannot receive a case until credentialing verifies them. This runs independently of any single case and feeds the assignment gate.

Always on

The rails that run under every step

These systems are not stages — they operate continuously alongside the whole journey, and are how the platform stays accountable, safe, and recoverable.

Immutable audit

Every action writes an append-only audit event: identity, role, case, entity, action, outcome, reason, correlation ID, and time.

audit_events

Status history

Each transition is recorded with its from/to states and reason — a full, replayable case timeline.

case_status_history

Notification outbox

The one-time code and staff alerts go through a transactional outbox with retries, backoff, and dead-lettering. No clinical narrative in messages.

notification_outbox

Secure messages

Four role-scoped threads connect patient↔coordinator and coordinator↔doctor/operations/finance. Bodies are encrypted; internal threads stay hidden from patients.

case_messages · case_message_reads

Blocking tasks

Versioned, encrypted work items support ownership, priority, due dates, start/complete/cancel/reassign, overdue state, and protected-transition blocking.

case_tasks

Private documents

Random object keys, presigned short-lived access, quarantine-then-scan, and audited downloads only for clean objects.

medical_documents
