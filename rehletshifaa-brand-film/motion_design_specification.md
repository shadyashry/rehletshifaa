# Motion design specification

## Architecture: why it renders identically every time
- **Framework.** HyperFrames 0.8.168. Each edition and language is one root composition (`source/master-{en,ar}.html`) generated by `tools/build_film.mjs`.
- **Timeline.** One paused GSAP timeline is registered at `window.__timelines[id]`. Each scene is a `<section class="clip scene">` with `data-start` and `data-duration`. Its builder `RS.SCENES[id](ctx)` adds tweens at `ctx.at(localTime)`.
- **Determinism.** No `Math.random` and no `Date.now`. All "life" comes from seeded PRNGs (mulberry32) and value noise keyed on time. Every pose, camera, walk and parallax value is a pure function of the timeline time, so any seek renders the same frame.
- **Rig driver.** One driver tween per scene (`RS.driveScene`) calls `rig.pose(t)` on every rig. Rigs evaluate keyframe *tracks* rather than stacking GSAP tweens per joint, which keeps hundreds of acting keys cheap and seek-safe.

## Character rig (`source/shared/rs-rig.js`, `rs-face.js`, `chars/*.js`)
- **Construction.** Layered parametric SVG in a 3/4 view, mirrored with `flip`.
- **Joints.** Neck, shoulders, elbows and wrists for both arms, plus hip-relative lean, shrug and breath.
- **Face**
  - Eyes: an iris and pupil follow gaze (`lookX`, `lookY`).
  - Lids: `lidL` and `lidR`, plus blinks and auto-blinks with "quiet" windows during intense moments.
  - Brows: `browRaise`, `browInner`, `browKnit`.
  - Mouth: `smile`, `mouthOpen`, and visemes via `RS.speak`.
  - Head: `headNod`, `headTilt`, `headTurn`.
  - Cheeks lift with the smile; the squint is a separate parameter.
- **Expression presets** (`RS.EXPRESSIONS`): neutral, concerned, attentive, thoughtful, reassured, warm, listening. They blend, never swap.
- **Hands.** Procedural fingers with curl, spread, index and thumb parameters, plus named poses: rest, hold, phone, point, open, loose, type. They interpolate between poses.
- **Procedural layers**, seeded per character: breath, a slow sway and micro-saccades.
- **Props in hand space** (`RS.attach`): pen, tablet, phone and suitcase handle follow the hand exactly.
- **Desk staging** (`RS.seatAtDesk`). The depth order is far arm → torso clipped at the desk edge → desk → near arm, so forearms rest *on* the surface.
- **Standing and walking** (`rs-standing.js`)
  - The grafted lower body (cardigan hem, wide-leg trousers, flats) pivots at the hips.
  - `RS.walk` is a pure-function walk: opposed leg swing of ±12°, a vertical bob at foot contact, hem sway, and a counter-swinging free arm. It eases in and out over 0.5 s.
  - It is framed knees-up so the wide-leg drape carries the gait.

## Acting grammar
Every beat follows **anticipation → action → response → settle**:
- **Eyes lead, head follows** by about 0.2–0.3 s. Example: the Consultant looking up to the imaging.
- **Breath before emotion.** Example: the scene 6 inhale, then the exhale on "The decision is yours".
- **Reactions are small.** Acceptance is a 3–6° nod and a 0.3–0.5 smile, never a grin.
- **Hands do things.** They read with a finger, write, type only when typing is heard, hold the phone and tablet, and pull the case.
- **Blinks are motivated.** They fall on cuts in thought and after a decision, with quiet windows while concentrating.

## Camera and transitions
- **Camera.** `RS.camAt(scale, cx, cy)` frames a world point at screen centre; `RS.parallax` scales the move per layer depth (far 0.45–0.6, mid 0.7–0.8, near 1).
- **Shot grammar.** Motivated pull-backs reveal people (scenes 3 and 4), slow push-ins create intimacy (scenes 1 and 6), and a tracking shot follows the walk (scene 8). Cut-ins use a 0.12 s paper breath (scene 9).
- **Object-led transitions**
  - The phone confirmation pushes into the office monitor.
  - The office case matches the consultant's tablet.
  - The video tile opens into the end card.
- **Scene overlaps.** `RS.TRANSITION_IN` sets 0.7–1.0 s per scene; the incoming shell fades over the outgoing one.

## UI motion (`rs-ui.js`, `rs-proposal.js`)
- **Site-accurate components.** Header, stepper, file rows with progress bars, checks, pills, buttons, chat bubbles, stamp, and the logo lockup.
- **Reveal order.** Elements reveal in reading order with a 10–16 px offset that mirrors in RTL. Durations are 0.35–0.55 s on `power2.out`.
- **Rewards.** Checks and confirmed pills get one `back.out` pop.
- **Typing.** A growing clip rect reveals the text, synced with the typing Foley.

## Titles, captions, rail
- **Titles** (`RS.TITLE_AT`, `RS.TITLE_OUT`): an eyebrow, a coral rule, the title, and the journey rail with a pulsing active node. They come in on the first spoken phrase and go out before any collision with UI.
- **Captions.** HTML, balanced chunking timed to word boundaries; there is never more than one on screen. They are suppressed over the end card (`RS.CAPTION_UNTIL`).

## Timing per language
`tools/build_timing.py` derives each scene's duration from its narration line (lead + speech + tail, with a base minimum). Scene modules use local times and `ctx.dur`, so the same choreography lands on each language's own narration.

## Performance
- **Speed.** Renders run on Chrome headless shell with beginframe and software GPU at roughly 3–4× slower than realtime per 1080p30 minute in this container.
- **Filter use.** Gaussian blur is used only for the out-of-focus background travellers in scene 8.
