# Creative direction — "From Uncertainty to Clarity"

## The film in one sentence
One woman's journey from a worrying knee report to a confirmed plan of care. It shows what RehletShifaa actually does, in the order it actually happens, and promises nothing it cannot keep.

## Audience and promise
- **Patients and families** considering care in Egypt. They need to feel *understood* before they feel *sold to*.
- **Partners and referrers** need to see a credible, role-clear process: who does what, and when.
- **Promise.** Understand your options before you travel: the brand line, and the live site's own positioning.

## Story spine (10 scenes, about two minutes per language)

| # | Beat | Emotional state | What the viewer learns |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Uncertainty at home | heavy, quiet | The problem is real and personal |
| 2 | Discover and send the case | first possibility | You start with what you already have |
| 3 | The Coordinator | someone has it in hand | One dedicated point of contact |
| 4 | The Consultant reviews | focus, care | A verified specialist reviews and recommends |
| 5 | Clarity before commitment | understanding | Recommendation, next steps, preliminary *range*, then a question answered |
| 6 | The decision | breath, agency | **The decision is hers** |
| 7 | Appointments, then travel | order | Medical appointments first; travel support is optional, chosen by her |
| 8 | Arrival | relief | The practical details are organized |
| 9 | Care | warmth | The treating team confirms the plan *after* assessment; the Coordinator stays close |
| 10 | Follow-up, then the brand | resolution | Support continues after she is home → logo, tagline, CTA |

## Characters (all fictional)
- **Amal M.** (mid-50s), the patient, with a knee concern. Dignified, practical, a little tired at the start, at ease by the end.
- **Nour** (early 30s), Patient Coordinator. Organised, warm, never pushy.
- **The Consultant** (around 50), orthopaedics. Attentive, unhurried, explains rather than pronounces.

There is no real patient data anywhere. The case number, dates and prices are illustrative and labelled as such on screen ("Illustrative example").

## Look
- **Petrol & Paper**, the live site's own palette. Paper backgrounds, petrol accents, and coral only for the brand dot, rules and tags. Exact tokens are in `brand_motion_guidelines.md`.
- **Editorial 2.5D vector illustration.** Soft gradients, a single warm key light per room, generous negative space. On-screen UI matches the website's components (cards, pills, buttons, stepper), so the film and the product feel like one brand.
- **Typography from the site.** Alexandria for headings and the wordmark, Plus Jakarta Sans for body text, Cairo for Arabic body text.

## Tone rules
- Calm, specific and humane. No stock "smiling doctor" clichés, no dramatic music, no countdowns.
- Show process, not superlatives.
- Arabic is a native production, not a translation. It has its own script in فصحى مبسطة, its own timing and an RTL layout.

## Claims guardrails (non-negotiable)
1. The preliminary estimate is a **range**, shown with "Non-binding. The final quote follows your in-person assessment." It is never shown as a final price.
2. The in-person assessment comes **before** the final care plan. Scene 9's plan is labelled "Confirmed after in-person assessment".
3. Travel is **optional** and arranged **only after** the patient's decision. In scene 7, flights and visa stay unticked; only the two services she asked for are selected.
4. No guaranteed outcomes and no diagnosis claims. RehletShifaa is never presented as owning a hospital; clinical responsibility stays with the treating team.
5. The decision is always the patient's ("You Decide").

## Call to action
- **EN.** "Send my case" → dev.rehletshifaa.com/en/send-my-case. This is the live site's label, so the viewer finds the same words on the page the URL opens; the brief's "Start My Case" is recorded as an alternative.
- **AR.** "أرسل حالتي" → dev.rehletshifaa.com/ar/send-my-case.
