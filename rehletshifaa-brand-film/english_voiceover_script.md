## Claim guardrails applied

- "preliminary costs" — never "price" or "quote"; the final quote follows the in-person assessment (site copy).
- "recommends appropriate next steps" — no diagnosis or outcome claims.
- Travel is conditional ("If you continue…") and follows the medical appointments.
- "Your treating team confirms the appropriate care plan" — clinical responsibility stays with the treating facility.
